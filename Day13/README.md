# Day 13 of 90: Session Cookies & Authentication Risks

- **Challenge:** Inspected browser cookies on a daily-use site to see how session persistence works under the hood.
- **My Focus:** Analyzed cookie attributes, session tracking, and the security implications of session hijacking.
- **Key Takeaway:** Cookies are small text files that remember your session, but if stolen, they let an attacker bypass multi-factor authentication entirely.
