# Day 12 of 90: HTTPS Certificates & The Browser Padlock

- **Challenge:** Inspected browser security certificates and broke down what the HTTPS padlock actually proves versus what people assume.
- **My Focus:** Analyzed encryption layers, Certificate Authorities, and the reality that malicious actors also use HTTPS.
- **Key Takeaway:** The padlock proves the connection is encrypted, not that the site is safe—about 71% of malware now arrives over HTTPS.
